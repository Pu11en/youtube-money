# Channel bible: lost tombs & history mysteries (Shorts)

Written 2026-09-19 from snapshots 4448ce5a8da90300, fd8660a0600745a2 and the transcripts of the

## The niche in numbers
- History-mystery Shorts this month: **median 1.3M views** across the top 25 (true crime 62K,
  science 84K). The "lost tomb" corner: Cleopatra 3.1M / 26 s on a **26.8K-sub** channel; Genghis
  Khan 2K–41K on channels with **8–1,500 subs** (a stickman one hit 9.8K on 97 subs).
- Interaction rate median 2.7% (highest of the three niches). All 25 winners under 4 minutes.
- Cleopatra is saturated (8 of the top 12 lost-tomb videos). Genghis, Alexander, Attila, Alaric,
  the First Emperor's sealed chamber, Nefertiti, Boudica, Cleopatra's *searcher* angle: open.

## Style DNA of the winners (structure we copy; wording we never copy)
- **Claim in the first 2 seconds.** "X has never been found." No greeting, no "did you know".
- **Fast:** 3.4–4.1 words per second; a 30 s Short carries 100–125 words. Our auto-run was 2.0 wps
  — half speed — which is why it felt empty.
- **Escalation chain:** each beat tops the last with "then… then… and then…" (soldiers killed →
  their killers killed → horses trample it → river diverted). 4–6 rungs.
- **One human detail** the viewer has never heard (the baby camel and its mother; a lawyer who
  spent 20 years digging). This is the share trigger.
- **A modern hunt** in the second half (satellites, a named searcher, a forbidden zone) — the
  3.1M video is *about the woman searching*, not the queen.
- **End on the standing mystery** in one line ("still lost", "no one may dig"). No call to action.
- **Title = a first-person or present-tense line**, not a topic: "I swear no one will ever find my
  grave" beats "The mystery of Cleopatra's tomb" by 30×.
- Length: 26–45 s wins; 6 s and 60+ s lose.

## Format rules for our videos
- 9:16, 30–40 s, 9–11 scenes, 3.5 wps → 105–140 words, **9–12 words per scene** (Veo scenes with
  spoken lines: 7 words max on a 4 s clip).
- One recurring narrator character (the horseman sheet, locked), one world, flat 2D doodle look.
- Captions bottom third, never over a face. Music low. No on-screen text baked into pictures.
- Facts: legend lines are flagged as legend in the card; anything presented as fact gets a source.
- Rules that never move: no finance/health/legal/politics; structure copied, never wording;
  nothing posts before Drew watches it.
