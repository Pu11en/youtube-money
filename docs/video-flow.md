# How one video gets made, step by step

## The big idea
- **This folder is the machine.** You talk in plain words in a Discord thread; the session follows one skill per step.
- **Any niche works** because the niche lives in a **channel profile**, not in the skills. Same skills run a history channel, a finance channel or a horror channel.
- **Every step ends with one saved file.** That file is the only input the next step needs, so a fresh session (Claude, Codex or DeepSeek) can pick up anywhere.
- **Every step has a "done when" check.** If the check fails, the step isn't finished and the next step refuses to start.
- **You only type short things:** a niche, a letter to pick, "ok", or a fix in your own words.
- ⚠️ **Credits get spent in only 3 places:** a 3-scene test, the full render, the thumbnail. Each needs your OK and a credit limit first.
- ✅ **Say "status" any time** and the session reads the files and tells you which step each video is on and what it needs from you.

## Part 1: Set up a channel (once per niche, all free)
### Step 1: Channel profile
- **You say:** "New channel: true crime from the 1800s" (any niche).
- **It asks you, one at a time:** who watches, long videos or Shorts, 2–5 channels you admire, narrator mood (calm, dramatic, funny), the look (painted, cartoon, photo-real), how the channel makes money.
- **You get:** a one-page **channel profile** card to approve.
- **Done when:** niche, audience, format, look and at least 2 competitor channels are filled in.
- **Hands to next step:** the competitor channel links.
### Step 2: Find the competitors' breakout videos
- **It does:** lists every video on each competitor channel with its views (free, no Blotato needed), then finds **"outliers"**, videos that got far more views than that channel's normal.
- **You get:** a lettered list of 5–10 outliers, like "C) 'The Village That Vanished' — 2.1M views, 9× this channel's normal".
- **You say:** "keep A, C, D, F".
- **Done when:** 3–5 outliers are picked.
- **Hands to next step:** the picked video links.
### Step 3: Style DNA
- **It does:** pulls the transcripts of those videos (free) and measures how they're built: how fast the title is confirmed, hook type, how often a question is teased and answered, sentence length, where the surprises land, how it ends.
- **You get:** a **style DNA** card of numbers and patterns, **never their sentences**.
- **You say:** "ok" or "make it less dramatic".
- **Done when:** every rule on the card points to which sample video it came from.
- **Hands to next step:** the style DNA, reused for every future video on this channel.

## Part 2: Pick the video and get the facts (free)
### Step 4: Ideas
- **You say:** "Ideas for true crime" or "ideas like outlier C".
- **It does:** writes 10 ideas that fit the channel and don't repeat a competitor's or our own earlier storylines.
- **You get:** a lettered list. Each idea has a title, a one-line promise, and **the wrong belief most viewers hold** (the opening trick that works best).
- **You say:** "D".
- **Done when:** one idea is chosen and a folder for this video exists.
- **Hands to next step:** the title, promise and wrong belief.
### Step 5: Facts
- **It does:** uses Blotato's research (free) and web sources to gather surprising, checkable facts.
- **You get:** a numbered fact list, **each with its source link**. Anything without a source is marked ⚠️ and can't be used.
- **You say:** "drop 4 and 9" or "ok".
- **Done when:** at least 8 sourced facts and no unsourced ones.
- **Hands to next step:** the approved fact list.

## Part 3: Write it (free)
### Step 6: Outline
- **It does:** turns the idea, facts and style DNA into beats. Each beat is linked by **"but" or "therefore"**, never "and then". It keeps a list of open questions so every question asked gets answered.
- **You get:** a short outline: hook, promise, 5–8 beats, the big reveal, ending.
- **You say:** "ok" or "move the reveal later". **This is the cheapest place to change the story.**
- **Done when:** every open question has a planned answer.
- **Hands to next step:** the approved outline.
### Step 7: Script draft and grading
- **It does:** writes the script in passes (structure → spoken draft → add specifics → strip the AI tone), then a **grader** scores it on 8 checks from proven creators, plus an originality check.
- **Rules of the grader:** harsh scores, **7 is good, 10 doesn't exist**. Below 7, it rewrites itself, up to 3 times.
- **You get:** the script, its score, and the top 3 fixes it already made.
- **You say:** "ok", or a fix in your words like "the ending drags".
- **Done when:** score is 7 or more and every fact in it is on the fact list.
- **Hands to next step:** the final script.
### Step 8: Your first OK (gate 1)
- **It asks:** "Approve this script, and what's the most credits this video may use?" It shows a cost estimate for 2–3 picture-quality levels.
- **You say:** "ok, 1,500 credits".
- **Done when:** your OK and the credit limit are saved with the exact script you approved. If the script changes after this, the OK is void.

## Part 4: Make the video (credits start here)
### Step 9: Scene plan
- **It does:** splits the script into scenes. Each scene gets one voice line and one picture prompt. Every picture prompt starts with the same **"locked style" sentence** so all pictures look like one channel.
- **You pick:** the voice (20 free choices) and the picture model (cheap test quality vs best quality).
- **Done when:** every script line belongs to exactly one scene and the cost estimate is under your limit.
- **Hands to next step:** the scene list.
### Step 10: 3-scene test (small spend)
- **It does:** renders just 3 scenes on Blotato and posts the clip in the thread.
- **You say:** "ok", or "too dark", "wrong voice". Fixes change the locked style sentence, and it tests again.
- **Done when:** you like the look and the voice.
### Step 11: Full render
- **It does:** renders the rest in batches of up to 20 scenes (Blotato's limit per video), then stitches them with captions. If stitching fails, it uses the free local assembler from Cinco Vid.
- **Checks credits before every batch** and stops if the next batch would pass your limit.
- **Done when:** one finished video file exists and credits used are logged.
### Step 12: Thumbnail, title and description
- **It does:** offers 3 thumbnail ideas in words first (free). After you pick one, it makes it with Nano Banana on Blotato. Title and description come from the script.
- **You say:** "B".
- **Done when:** thumbnail, title and description are saved.
### Step 13: Your second OK (gate 2)
- **It does:** posts the video and thumbnail in the thread.
- **You say:** "post it", or a fix like "scene 14 looks wrong". Fixes re-render **only those scenes**, not the whole video.
- **Done when:** your OK is saved against the exact video file you watched.

## Part 5: Post and learn
### Step 14: Post
- **It does:** posts to YouTube through Blotato with the thumbnail, title, description and playlist, or schedules it for the time you say.
- **Done when:** the YouTube link is saved in the video folder.
### Step 15: Learn from results
- **You say:** "results for true crime", and paste the views, watch time and where people left (Blotato can't read YouTube numbers, so you copy them from YouTube Studio).
- **It does:** compares the result to the plan: did people leave at the hook, a slow beat, the ending?
- **You get:** 1–3 lessons that get added to the channel profile, so the next video starts smarter.
- **Done when:** the lessons are saved.

## How it fits together
### The files (one saved file per step)
- **Channel folder:** profile → competitor outliers → style DNA → lessons.
- **Video folder:** idea → facts → outline → script + grade → your OK + credit limit → scene plan → test clip → video → thumbnail + title → your OK → YouTube link → results.
### The skills (one per step, about 9 in total)
- **Channel setup** (steps 1–3), **ideas** (4), **facts** (5), **writer + grader** (6–7), **approval gate** (8 and 13), **scene planner** (9), **render** (10–11), **thumbnail + title** (12), **post + learn** (14–15), plus a **status** skill that reads the files.
### What already exists vs what's new
- ✅ **Already found:** the proven prompt kit for style DNA, writing and grading; Blotato's research, render, stitch and post; the Cinco Vid assembler; OpenCLI logged into Blotato's website.
- ⬜ **New to build:** the skills themselves, the file layout, the credit checks, and the status reader.
- ⚠️ **Never tested live yet:** Blotato's scene-video template and stitching 8 batches into one long video. The 3-scene test in step 10 is where we find out.
