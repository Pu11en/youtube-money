> [80:10] "my intro formula is like immediately try to deliver on the promise in in some sense. [...] just make your viewer feel like they made the right decision. ### Foreshadowing: the "made-up focus group" and "Soon X, first Y" (source 1)
> [60:31] "I call it like the invisible or the madeup focus group where it's like if we pause a video, let's say at the 1 minute mark, 2 minute mark, 4-minute mark [...] if we put our audience into a focus group at this point, what would they say is coming up in the video? [...] I like foreshadowing that creates this like exact expectation of what I'm going to get in the rest of the video."

> [62:20] "I like this idea of like soon X first Y [...] you say a little bit later, I'm going to ask you about this, but first this. And the key is that your X and your Y both need to be interesting."

> [63:43] "I like to think about every video [...] as this skeleton ahead of time [...] And then I look at that skeleton, I put it in front of me as a one pager and I'm like where am I inserting little like retention devices? So I could say in the first two minutes I'm going to insert um some foreshadowing of like a question we're going to ask later. Maybe at the five or six minute mark, I'm gonna like [...] throw this like curveball in"

> [61:54] "the payoff [...] can be completion. So [...] the complete guide to YouTube [...] when you finish it, you're going to have this full playbook"

> [64:36] "I like chapters for education, tutorial, listicles, podcast. I don't like them for anything that's like narrative or like story based cuz I just feel like it just incentivizes people to like skip to the big payoff."

### "Puzzle list" structure (source 2)
> [33:01] "basically every video would follow a listical format, but it was like a puzzle list. So you'd piece uh pieces of the puzzle together to give the full picture. [...] the first thing he did was this. But without this other thing that we're going to talk about next, it wouldn't have made sense. [...] instead of it being like how do [they] grow random tip random tip random tip cuz that's like a bit chaotic and hard to to follow. So it's like building a story into a list"
