## Verbatim
The 8 steps (Channel 101 wording as widely reproduced):
> "1. A character is in a zone of comfort, 2. But they want something. 3. They enter an unfamiliar situation, 4. Adapt to it, 5. Get what they wanted, 6. Pay a heavy price for it, 7. Then return to their familiar situation, 8. Having changed."

Harmon, Adult Swim video:
> [00:00] "the so-called story circle is my attempt to remove all of the hard and repeated work from the task of breaking a story we do an eight-step story process here I call it an embryo you've got a character that you can identify with it has some kind of need wish incompletion causes them to go across a threshold where the story changes direction they go down a road of trials searching for something they find it whether they like it or not it kind of kicks their ass and they come back to the world they started in having changed"

## Pipeline takeaway
Useful as an optional `macro_arc` template for documentary and biography videos about a person or company, e.g. a company that "wants something, crosses a threshold, pays a price". It fits curiosity explainers built around a question less well. There, the Harris promise→answer arc and the MrBeast minute-map fit better.
