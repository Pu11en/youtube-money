# The exact flow from the Sollo videos

## Where this comes from
- **The Short you sent:** "EXPOSING How Much YouTube Pays in 2026" by One Person Business (1:44). It only lists the steps, so I also pulled the full tutorial it points to.
- **The full tutorial:** "I Cloned a $54,227/Month Faceless Channel Only Using Claude + Sollo AI" (12 min, June 2026, about 82k views). The host is Dave, and he rebuilds a dinosaur channel called **Extinct Zoo** step by step.
- **Everything below is from the transcripts** of both videos: what he types, what he pastes, and what comes out.
- ⚠️ **His actual prompts aren't in the video.** He keeps them in a free Notion page inside his community (Classroom → AI systems → "Sollo AI + Claude"). We only know what each prompt asks for, not its wording.
- ⚠️ **It's an ad:** Sollo pays him as an affiliate, and the $32k and $54k numbers can't be checked.

## Steps 1–3: Claude does the thinking
### Step 1: Build the channel "brain"
- **He does:** in Claude he makes a new **Project** named after the channel ("Extinct Zoo").
- **He pastes:** a ready-made prompt from his Notion page into the Project's instructions box, with his niche filled in ("dinosaurs and prehistoric animals"). Then he saves.
- **He gets:** a Claude Project that always knows what the channel is about. Every later chat happens inside it.
- **Feeds step 2:** the niche.
### Step 2: Ideas from the competitor
- **He does:** screenshots the competitor's **most popular videos** page and attaches it to a new chat in that Project.
- **He pastes:** his "ideas" prompt. It asks Claude to **work out why these videos work** and give new ideas built on the competitor's **title patterns**, without copying them.
- **He gets:**
  - the title formulas the competitor uses
  - why their thumbnails get clicks
  - thumbnail text ideas
  - **15 ideas ranked by likely click rate**
- **He picks one:** "The sea monster that hunted the megalodon".
- **Feeds steps 3 and 7:** the title, plus the thumbnail-text advice.
### Step 3: Facts nobody knows
- **He pastes:** his "facts" prompt plus the title. It asks for **little-known details most people haven't heard**, because generic info doesn't get pushed by YouTube.
- **He gets:** **10 unique points** about the topic.
- **Feeds step 4:** those 10 points.

## Steps 4–7: Sollo makes the pieces
### Step 4: The script (Sollo Script Writer)
- **He fills in a form:**
  - what the video is about, and the title
  - **details:** pastes the 10 points from Claude (it can also take PDFs or YouTube links)
  - **writing style:** pastes the **competitor channel's link** so Sollo copies how they write (or describes the style in words)
  - **money goal:** "ad revenue", meaning maximize watch time
  - **length:** up to 16 minutes; he picks 10 ("longer videos do better")
- **He clicks** "generate script" and gets a finished script in one go. No grading and no rewriting.
- **Feeds steps 5 and 6:** the script.
### Step 5: Pictures (Sollo Scene Generator)
- **He pastes:** the whole script.
- **He picks:** how many scenes (**50** here, up to 150) and a look, **"cinematic 2D animation"**, to match the competitor.
- **He gets:** 50 pictures that follow the script, with **the same characters throughout**. They download **numbered 1, 2, 3…** so they're already in order.
### Step 6: Voiceover (Sollo Voiceover Studio)
- **He pastes:** the script and picks one of about 9,500 voices, then downloads the audio.
- **His advice:** **record your own voice if you can.** With everyone using AI voices, a real voice or accent stands out and builds a connection.
### Step 7: Thumbnail (Sollo Thumbnail Maker)
- **He pastes:** the link to one of the **competitor's videos** and uses "edit from YouTube".
- **He types:** the change Claude suggested in step 2, "change the text to BIGGER THAN".
- **He gets:** a remade thumbnail to download.

## Step 8: Put it together (CapCut, by hand)
- **He does:** imports the voiceover onto the timeline, then imports the numbered pictures and lines each one up with the voice.
- **He admits** this is the slow part ("takes a little bit of time"). Sollo says it will do this automatically soon.
- **He gets:** the finished video to upload.

## Steps 9–10: After posting
### Step 9: Why didn't it get views? (Sollo Performance Analyzer)
- **He pastes:** his own video's link and clicks "collect data". It looks at the pictures, what's said and the comments.
- **He adds:** a **screenshot of the video's YouTube Studio numbers**. More numbers make the diagnosis more accurate.
- **He gets:**
  - why it's not getting views
  - how much money it missed
  - **new titles and thumbnail text to test**
  - **a to-do checklist** for the next video
### Step 10: Bonus, a product to sell
- **He pastes:** a channel link and a screenshot of its videos, then clicks "build it for me".
- **He gets:** a full **PDF guide and a sales page**. He sells it on **Gumroad** (free to use) for about **$29**, linked in the bio and video descriptions.
- **His examples:** faceless channels selling PDF guides for $17–$47 on top of ad money.

## What this means for our version
- **Steps 1–3 are just Claude, which we already are.** The Discord thread takes the place of his Claude Project, and the channel profile takes the place of his instructions box.
- **Step 4 is where Sollo is weakest:** one click, no fact checks, no grading. Our style DNA, writer and grader beat it, and never copy wording.
- **Steps 5–7 map to Blotato:** the scene video template (up to 20 scenes each, stitched together), its 20 free voices, and Nano Banana for thumbnails.
- **Step 8 we automate:** Blotato stitches the video, or the free local assembler from Cinco Vid does it. No CapCut.
- **Steps 9–10 are new ideas for our flow:** a "why didn't it work" review from a Studio screenshot, and a product builder. We didn't have the product builder planned yet.
- ⚠️ **Two differences from his flow:** he uses 50 pictures for 10 minutes (about 12 seconds each), and he tells people to record their own voice. Both matter for YouTube's rule against samey AI videos.
